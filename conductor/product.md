# Product Guide: Tower Finder 4900

## Vision & Overview
Tower Finder 4900 is an advanced tower detection and CRM dashboard designed to streamline tower location management and owner lookup processes. It empowers internal operations and management/sales teams with efficient search capabilities, highly accurate data, and robust lead management workflows.

## Target Audience
- **Internal Operations:** Users managing tower assets and coordinating field data.
- **Management/Sales:** Teams tracking leads and performance throughout the tower acquisition or maintenance pipeline.

## Core Goals
- **Efficiency in Search:** Rapidly identify and visualize tower locations via an interactive map.
- **Data Accuracy:** Ensure high-quality owner and parcel information through automated data lookups.
- **Lead Management:** Accelerate the transition from initial lead identification to actionable opportunities.

## Primary Features
- **Interactive Map Integration:** Real-time visualization of tower locations with status tracking.
- **CRM & Activity Tracking:** Comprehensive management of notes, follow-up activities, and call tracking.
- **Automated Data Lookup:** seamless integration with NRCan, parcel data, and geocoding services.
- **User Management:** Role-based access control to protect sensitive tower and owner information.

## Technical Constraints & Considerations
- **High Performance Search:** The dashboard must handle large datasets efficiently, ensuring fast map rendering and search results.
- **Security & Permissions:** Robust protection of tower and owner data is paramount.
- **Responsive UI:** A seamless experience across various screen sizes to support diverse user workflows.
